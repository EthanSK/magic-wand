# R47 complete plate trial

On 17 November 2026 the owner reported that the first print finished successfully. The running job carried the R47 complete-assembly name and the native archive prefix `b13dee0f`. The nine-part print-only package and its source archive are identified in the [delivery receipt](exports/complete-plate/print-only.json).

During cleanup, the owner found one half of the key holder released easily. Supports on the other half stuck in recesses and pulling them out broke or damaged features. The affected side and the exact failing support have not been identified. The OP1 harness pulled or bent between its connection and the wire opening. The owner also reported that the OP1 holder snapped near the bottom of a triangular section. Its precise fracture surface has not been identified. With one hinge tab inserted, the other did not reach the opposite receiver. The owner identified insufficient arm spread and outward pressure. The complete assembly has yet to demonstrate a reliable click and release.

The original battery holder and its removable retainer were reported to assemble and hold well. The narrower Micron cell belongs to the next design. Fine, removable hairs appeared on several small red parts. The cause was not established from the finished parts.
