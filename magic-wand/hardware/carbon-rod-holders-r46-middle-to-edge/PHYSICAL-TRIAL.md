# R46 physical trial

The successful retry retained native archive SHA-256 `3d0267fb983986b44cd6a90b5eb3083ce77dfe3be016ea58887e7dd864cfa157`. It printed only the key carrier with its removable helpers and timelapse tower. The controller later reported all 209 layers complete, no active errors, and both heater targets off. This terminal read does not establish continuous camera coverage of the whole run.

On 17 November 2026 the operator reported that the carrier mostly succeeded. Its button/mounting holes fit, and the support frame could be removed, but some contacts were difficult and left snapped-off nubs. The OP1 board also catches the lower lip despite its mounting holes lining up. Treat hole alignment, whole-board seating and easy support removal as separate results.

The DFRobot hinged bracket and the other holders were not in this key-holder-only delivery. The complete hinge, click travel, fitted board retention, comfort and breaking force have not been physically verified by this trial. Preserve this printed carrier as a baseline while the next revision changes the lower click mount and support contacts.

The earlier canceled first-layer attempt remains a separate trial with an unresolved cause. Its low-angle appearance alone did not establish whether the base was attached; the successful retry does not prove what happened in that earlier attempt.
