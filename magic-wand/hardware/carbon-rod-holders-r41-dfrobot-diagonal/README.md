# R41 · DFRobot touch with diagonal OP1 click

R41 returns to the DFRobot DFR0030 V2 touch board and puts the OP1 click
under the three-key row. The touch board sits on R35's two-post hinged bracket with two
printed post clips. The OP1 PCB uses R32's 25° plan-view angle, so its switch
sits under the gold pad near the lower keys. The hinge stays at the two-key
end. Choc seats, key heights, the rod clip and the finger guards match R39.

Generated by `holder41.py` (solids and checks) and `assembly41.py` (exports,
manifest, BOM and guide). Run both through the repository's
`geometry_budget.py`. Nothing here talks to a printer.

## Changes from R39

- **Touch board:** DFRobot DFR0030 V2 on the R35 bracket, not the Grove board.
  The bracket gains R39's taller arm roots, 1.5 mm root fillets, squeeze stop
  and bridge, plus R38's adjustable M2 × 4 stop screw. Its pusher is a
  1.1 × 0.6 mm pad aligned with the carrier, inside the diagonal plunger.
  A small 45° reinforcement under the floor backs the pusher.
- **OP1 board:** turned 25° at R32's height, with the plunger at
  (−1.6, 3.05). The plunger is 28.1 mm from the hinge axis (R40: 16.15 mm).
  The whole OP1 group moves 1.6 mm left of R32. This keeps two printed lines
  (0.73 mm) between the board slot and the upper-right Choc clip relief, and
  1.48 mm at the plate.
- **OP1 harness:** fitted before the holder goes on the rod, the same order
  as the Choc sockets. The plug is pushed up from below. Its lead presses into
  an open underside groove beside the rod channel and leaves at the two-key
  end. Seated, the plug clears the rod by 0.31 mm.
- **Stop range:** the stop landing is 0.15 mm higher. The bracket's outer feet
  are relieved to 13.42 mm, so the order is: screw stop, then backup feet,
  then plunger bottoming. Fully in, the screw allows 0.73 mm nominal travel.
  Two turns out, it allows 0.08 mm. The backup feet stop at 0.75 mm, 0.09 mm
  before the modelled plunger meets the switch body.
- **Gravity socket pins:** the maker's photo shows the pins behind the
  side-entry housing, about 9 mm from the board end. A 45°-ended pocket over
  the inner tips of the root fans clears 1.5 and 2.0 mm tails by 0.23 mm. The
  check covers ±0.5 mm across and ±0.6 mm along the board, seated, while the
  board is loaded and through the click. The arm sections do not change.
- **Small repairs:**
  - R40's left guard web is trimmed flush with the guard face.
  - A 0.55 mm chamfer on the right hinge cap clears the Gravity lead.
  - Bosses, pilots and socket barrels have 45° teardrop print roofs.

## PETG trial preparation

`plate41.py` prepares all nine parts for the A1 mini, 0.4 mm nozzle and
textured PEI plate. The recipe is the R37 Flashforge HS PETG Metallic
baseline: 245°C nozzle, 60°C bed, 0.2 mm layers, slow first layers and
8 mm³/s maximum flow. The executable temperature waits are checked;
a PETG profile label alone is insufficient.

The carrier uses native snug Normal automatic supports at 45°, with four local
enforcers for the diagonal openings and guard. Supports may start on the
model, with a 0.2 mm top/bottom gap and 0.35 mm side gap. Native blockers keep
support out of the rod bore, both clip mouths and the two hinge bores; the
actual deposited paths must also pass the zero-support checks there. The rod ledge has actual
prior-layer backing rather than a redundant enforcer. The bracket has two local,
bed-only Normal support enforcers under its early
backup-stop feet, using the same 0.2 mm top gap. Each supported part is matched
to Studio's explicit object label and checked separately, including removal
access and spacing from the other supports and wipe tower. The bracket has
four variable-width walls and solid infill. Its minimum wall setting is
60% of the nozzle diameter, with width variation distributed over two
walls; actual deposition in the arms, posts and pusher must still pass
the material checks. Small post clips use 0.4 mm line widths.

Wide sacrificial pads with narrow cut ties anchor the tall parts; the
remaining parts have checked removable brims or pads. Smooth timelapse
uses a separate wipe tower. Remove that tower, pads, ties and supports
before fitting electronics. Supports occupy working openings until
cleanup; their reachable removal routes do not prove easy physical removal.

The pusher load path runs through its local floor reinforcement. The audit
checks the complete pusher, pusher-to-reinforcement joint, reinforcement,
and full interface with the original floor. It also retains the smaller
direct-only floor-contact measurement as a diagnostic, rather than hiding
the local edge underfill in that subset. No inset core or lower material
threshold substitutes for the complete load-path checks. Every intersecting
layer must carry each nominal interface in one actual deposited component.
The remaining root fans beside the Gravity-pin pocket are checked separately.
The report lists temporary support in every functional route and operating
pose, including all socket, pin, wire, pilot and flex spaces.

Generate with `assembly41.py`, prepare in a fresh scratch directory with
`plate41.py`, then run `audit41.py` on the actual sliced archive and that
staging directory. Use the repository's serial `geometry_budget.py` runner
for these jobs. Reopen and re-slice the passing archive once and audit its
own resulting paths before packaging with `plate41.py --package`.
The raw source STLs require this preparation and are not ready print files.

The retained `exports/print-trial/print-manifest.json` is the composite
nine-part staging manifest used by the slice audit, including reused parts.
It is distinct from `exports/manifest.json`, which records this revision's
locally generated meshes. Retained print anchors use the same staging frame.
Run `trial41.py` with the initial and reopened evidence directories to retain
the selected files and compose `trial-review.json`. It rejects stale source
or archive bindings and records every partial-overhang decision, precision
opening check and occupied cleanup route. The report describes the digital
trial at generation time; it does not track a live physical print.

## Limits

These are nominal CAD checks. Physical fit, click feel, strength, support
removal and comfort are untested. Several sizes are photo-derived or assumed:

- **DFR0030:** 35 mm length, hole pitch and position, 1.6 mm thickness, and
  the Gravity pin position and tail length.
- **OP1:** PCB, socket, plug and lead envelopes.

The click is a nominal geometric stop, not a measured actuation. Set the
stop screw against the real switch.
