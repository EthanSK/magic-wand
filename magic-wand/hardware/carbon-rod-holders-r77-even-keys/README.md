# R77 key holder

[Finger rests and sockets](../carbon-rod-holders/assembly/?version=r77&component=keys&view=finger_rest) · [Hinge and wiring](../carbon-rod-holders/assembly/?version=r77&component=keys&view=pin_hinge)

Both key banks have a 17.95 mm pitch and a 0.55 mm gap between neighbouring caps. Looking from the underside, the battery-side lower key and socket turn back 90° anticlockwise. Slide the wired black socket in from the outside, slightly towards the hinge, centre it below the contacts and then press upwards. The two long branches on each finger rest follow the same curves. The outer lobe of the smaller battery-side loop is open for assembly.

The three touchpad conductors leave the connector underneath the board and run lengthwise between the two matching hinge arms. The conductors rise over the pin clip. The pivot, click surface and retainer keep their earlier positions. These checks use the modelled wires and solder. Real wire dress, printed fit and comfort need a physical trial.

The rectangular shelf remaining from the older outboard battery rail has been removed. The pack sits 3.10 mm from the deck and 0.20 mm from the modelled soldered socket terminals. Keep those wires flat. The rail lips retain the pack. It slides out through the battery end.
