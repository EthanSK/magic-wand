# R79 replacement key holder

[Inspect the fixed hinge and clips](../carbon-rod-holders/assembly/?version=r79&component=keys&view=pin_hinge) · [See where to cut the base](exports/removing-the-base.png)

The fixed right pin hole on the R78 key holder split during removal of its disposable base. The hinge itself printed directly against the build plate. The print file had no automatic support under the hinge, but the nearby right shoulder had a much stronger connection to the disposable sheet than the left. The fracture surface was not available to establish whether the part split along a print layer.

Each R79 hinge ear has a short diagonal brace into the solid rim and a bevel on the face that touches the build plate. The pin holes, paddle seats and pin insertion path keep their fitted geometry. The narrow part of each disposable shoulder tab is 0.84 mm wide and 1.8 mm tall. An extra tab on the solid underside of the key bank holds the carrier steady during printing.

Both native slices put 1.31 mm² of plastic across the right shoulder connection, down from 5.59 mm² in R78. The left connection measures 1.26 mm². These areas describe the printed paths. The force needed to separate a finished part still needs a physical check.

## Removing the base

Let the build plate cool. Support the thick key-holder body while freeing it from the plate. Snip the six connections marked in the picture with flush cutters. Peel the two wings away from the holder. Keep fingers and tools off the thin walls around the pin holes while freeing the base.

## Fitting the touch board

Seat the board on its two posts. Slide each flat clip across the board with its opening toward the narrow part of the post, until the clip seats under the head. These are the original 1.4 mm clips used through R70. Their shorter height leaves up to 0.8 mm of vertical play before the head stops them. The current post ribs still locate the board sideways. Insertion force and board play need a physical check.

The replacement plate contains the carrier, both board clips, a fresh two-post bar, the OP1 retainer, the hinge pin, its retaining clip and a spare retaining clip. Reuse the R78 moving paddle. The assembly viewer includes it.
