# R76 click-end finger rest

[See the two guards with the seated battery and sockets](../carbon-rod-holders/assembly/?version=r76&component=finger_rest) · [Battery fitting and removal](../carbon-rod-holders/assembly/?version=r76&component=battery)

The index finger rests on the underside opposite the battery entrance. Both rod-side branches now follow the same three-dimensional curve, and the rounded loops above them share the same geometry. The battery-side loop sits 1.05 mm closer to the click so its rear support builds continuously from the deck. A direct copy of the outside branch overlaps the lower black key socket. That branch curves below the socket during side insertion, then rejoins the reference shape.

Fit and remove the battery through the rail entrance. About 2.5 mm of pack projects at that end so the guard can carry the matching loop. Push on the wrapped pack when removing it. The spring still holds the pack over the full rail length. Actual printed fit, comfort and retention need a fitted trial.

The two independent A1 mini slices have no floating starts. On the revised loop and brace, the longest run without contact with an earlier bead is 1.72 mm. All 336 switch/socket positions and seven OP1 fitting movements clear in the exported model.
