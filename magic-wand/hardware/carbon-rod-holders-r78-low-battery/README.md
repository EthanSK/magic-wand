# R78 key holder

[See the seated battery](../carbon-rod-holders/assembly/?version=r78&component=battery&view=battery_removal) · [Watch it slide in](../carbon-rod-holders/assembly/?version=r78&component=battery)

The integrated rails bring the 27 × 11.8 × 3.9 mm battery 0.8 mm closer to the keypad base than R77. Slide the disconnected pack in from the battery end with the leads free. Leave about 4.5 mm of the pack showing past the ends of the rails. The side spring grips the pack at that position. The raised end cap and the steps on the rail lips have been removed. Both lips are 1.6 mm thick throughout. The outer rail joins the base along a continuous roof. The new rail section adds about 0.25 cm³ of plastic.

The nearest battery face sits 2.30 mm from the base. Over the entire insertion path the model leaves 0.29 mm to the soldered terminal and 0.23 mm to the tape around the socket. Actual solder, tape thickness, and the fit of a printed holder still need a physical check.
