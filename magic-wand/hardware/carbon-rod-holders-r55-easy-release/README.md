# R55 easy-release key holder and complete hinge kit

R55 puts all seven functional pieces on one A1 mini plate. Keep the key holder, hinged paddle with its built-in pivot, sliding hinge cap, sliding OP1 board retainer, two-post DFRobot bar and both DFRobot clips. Each piece has its own wide removable base. Discard the separate timelapse tower. [Tray map](exports/tray.png)

R54 stayed attached through all 257 layers. The panel was hard to remove at the hinge. R55 opens the panel to the edge at the hinge and moves the two tabs off the hinge cheeks onto the underside of the rear rim. All six tabs are accessible along the rim. The two hinge cheeks have 3 mm of clearance from the base. The sliced holder-to-panel joint measures 25.02 mm² across 12 layers, compared with 24.57 mm² on R54. The functional meshes and their assembly steps come from R53. [Assembly steps](../carbon-rod-holders-r53-guided-op1/README.md)

## Removing the panel

Lift the cooled holder and panel off the sheet together. Snip the six green-marked tabs with flush cutters, flat face against the holder. Cut the outside pair underneath first, then the inside pair underneath and the two above the rim. Fold the panel wings away from the open hinge bay and lift the holder free.
