# Battery cradle

The original seven-piece print lost the upright cradle. On the printer recording it leaves its position around frame 100, lands beside the timelapse tower by frame 110, and strands then accumulate. The shorter first retry also came loose. It was in place at frame 14, moved in frames 15–16 and lay beside the tower by frame 17. The camera shows the part moving under the head and then across the sheet. The nozzle obscures the first point of release.

The replacement plate puts both long tray walls on the bed and widens the six removable outside pads. The first-layer area connected to the cradle rises from 346 to 549 mm². The separate pull-out support has another 247 mm² of its own contact. The tray floor now prints across three runners. Its longest stretch without support falls from 27.0 to 2.4 mm. The first retry had already moved before the floor printed.

The permanent geometry comes from the R48 Micron cradle. The tray reaches 17.6 mm and takes the same sliding lid. Bambu Studio estimates 33 min 54 s and 4.57 g of PETG, with the nozzle at 245 °C and textured bed at 60 °C. The replacement passed the same checks on a second slice. Adhesion and fit still need a physical trial. ## Removal and fit

1. Let the plate cool and lift the assembly. Snip the six narrow outside pad connections against the rigid walls.
2. Grip the wide tab at the open end and hold the tray by its side walls. Pull the tab straight out. The three long runners and the two shoulders under the lid guides come out together. If the tab sticks, ease its end away from the tray before pulling again. Keep the tool clear of the rod rings.
3. With the battery removed, slide the existing lid in and back out. Slide both rings over the free end of the carbon rod. Stop if they bind. Install the battery once the tray and guides are clear of any plastic that could rub the pouch. [Assembly guide for this cradle](../../../carbon-rod-holders-r48-edge-click/battery_guide48.py)
