# Battery cradle

The original seven-piece print lost the upright cradle. On the printer recording it leaves its position around frame 100, lands beside the timelapse tower by frame 110, and strands then accumulate. The shorter first retry also came loose. It was in place at frame 14, moved in frames 15–16 and lay beside the tower by frame 17. The camera shows the part moving under the head and then across the sheet. The nozzle obscures the first point of release.

The current plate puts both long tray walls on the bed and joins them to two 0.4 mm thick outside panels through six cut ties. The area connected to the cradle is 3,103 mm², up from 346 mm² on the failed short retry. The pull-out fork has its own 3,116 mm² base. The two sets stay separate. The longest unsupported stretch across the tray floor is 2.4 mm. The earlier retry had already moved before that floor printed.

The permanent geometry comes from the R48 Micron cradle and takes the same lid. Bambu Studio estimates 56 min and 8.99 g of PETG for the 17.6 mm tall print. The two independent slices give the same fitted passages and positive connections on both base layers. The six outside ties measure 2.1–2.3 mm wide and the two fork ties measure 1.8 mm. Physical adhesion and fit still need a trial. [Print-only file](R52-Battery-Cradle-Reinforced-Tower-print-only.3mf) · [Editable layout](layout.3mf) · [Measurements](checks.json)

## Removal and fit

1. Let the plate cool and lift the assembly. Snip the three outside connections on each side to free the two wide panels. Cut the two narrow connections between the front panel and the pull tab while supporting the tab.
2. Grip the wide tab at the open end and hold the tray by its side walls. Pull the tab straight out. The three long runners and the two shoulders under the lid guides come out together. The fork and cradle panels come within 0.3 mm at the open end. If plastic has joined them there, separate the cooled seam before pulling the tab. Keep the tool clear of the rod rings.
3. With the battery removed, slide the existing lid in and back out. Slide both rings over the free end of the carbon rod. Stop if they bind. Install the battery once the tray and guides are clear of any plastic that could rub the pouch. [Assembly guide for this cradle](../../../carbon-rod-holders-r48-edge-click/battery_guide48.py)
