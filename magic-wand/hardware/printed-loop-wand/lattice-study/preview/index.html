Diamond net, Open helix and Cross braced are separate open-frame experiments. Leaf shell retains the full V18 spiral strips. All retain the original joints, recessed switch seats, battery guides and detachable-loop lock. The reinforced options taper their wide bands towards the tip and use a smaller open sensor cradle with the existing retaining clip and board pockets. The one-shaft version has its own smaller tip connector; use its matching cradle. Fit and strength still need a physical test. The baseline remains unchanged.
