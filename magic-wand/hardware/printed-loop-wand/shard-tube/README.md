# Sharper tube with XIAO at the tip

This prototype keeps the XIAO sensor at the tip. The two-wide-spiral tube is one piece, with the core tapering from 18 mm to 12.4 mm across. The existing 10.4 mm design bore remains; a 10 mm probe passes the checked source. The wider removable sensor holder overlaps the thin tube and uses two tabs and a 40-degree turn channel. The V20 grip, buttons, battery parts and rounded removable finger loop are unchanged. Earlier models remain available.

Use the new shaft and matching open cradle together. Their head joint differs from V18–V20 and the earlier one-piece variants. Geometry checks cover insertion, turn, axial capture, the board, solder, USB, wire passage and both handed grip interfaces. The actual sliced paths also check the holder socket, entry and turning channel. The lower collar has no internal support enforcer, so the turning channel stays clear. These checks do not prove physical fit, print quality, strength, repeated joint use or retention. Test the matching connector and holder before a full print.

The complete 3MF includes both handed grips and one set of the other parts; the optional one-left file contains one complete left wand. The 192 mm shaft fits the checked 250 mm P1S profile. Both projects use the P1S 0.4 mm PETG settings, 0.2 mm layers, three walls and the V20 solid loop at 100% infill. One left wand estimates 47.537 g of printed parts plus 3.794 g supports and brim. The wider holder estimates 4.669 g versus 2.848 g for the previous one-piece V19 holder; the V20 loop and other design changes also affect the total. Electronics and battery are additional. No print has been sent.

To reproduce, install `../v20/requirements.txt`, export `wand_cad.py` to a new scratch directory, copy the checked exports into `exports`, then run `verify_models.py`, `print_projects.py`, `check_sliced_paths.py` and `check_v20_grip_paths.py`. Record the outputs and use `verify_release.py`. The V20 grip and loop are referenced from their canonical source; they are not separately maintained copies.
