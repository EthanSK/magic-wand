# Print V20 — wider rounded handle, compatible with V19

The solid finger loop widens from **7 to 9 mm** across the fingers and remains **4 mm thick** in its plane. Its edges now have a **1.4 mm radius**, replacing the flat chamfers. A continuous rounded sweep with a **23 mm gradual flare** blends the band into each wider locking pad; the separate angular bridge is removed.

## Preserved fit and layout

The fitted seats, pegs, cam tracks, latch and release direction remain V19-compatible. Only **Removable_Finger_Loop** and **Loop_Foot_Coupon** change; the other sixteen canonical STL files are byte-identical to V19, including both handed stems, shafts, head, cap and switch parts. **A V19 wand needs only the replacement handle printed.** Test its fit before use.

The butt reference and all controls remain fixed: mount centres **14 / 104 mm**, buttons **111 / 107 / 81 mm**, mirrored angles for both hands. The rounded band reaches about 9.04 mm above the butt, with about 6.04 mm clearance above the visible cap. The slim hollow grip, original raised counter-wound helix, continuous taper, 410 mm overall length, curved windows, 10.4 mm cable bore, battery guides and rounded diamond remain unchanged. The wand has recessed mounts when the handle is removed.

## Material and checks

One complete left wand estimates **63.260 g printed parts**, plus **3.394 g removable support/brim**. V19 estimates 61.305 g printed parts: this wider, smoothly blended handle adds **1.955 g**. The loop itself estimates **9.399 g**. These are sliced extrusion estimates, excluding electronics, battery, bought switches, sockets, caps and wires. Hardware remains unweighed.

Prepared settings: P1S 0.4 mm, PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls; **100% infill on the solid loop and foot coupon**, retained 20% elsewhere. The loop prints broad plane horizontal, with removable normal supports below the band between the wider ends. Switch plates stay broad face down.

All eighteen meshes are closed, consistently wound and each one connected. Geometry checks cover both hands, full lift/turn/release routes, positive stops, capture, cap, cell, switches and board clearances. Sampled sliced paths check working passages. All seven prepared projects slice without reported warnings. The portable CAD reproduces all eighteen mesh bytes. Physical fit, comfort, strength and PETG fatigue remain unverified; use the small fit test first. The battery envelope remains provisional at 20 × 6 × 40 mm and must be measured against the actual cell.
