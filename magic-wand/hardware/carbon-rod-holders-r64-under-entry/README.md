# R64 complete key holder

## Assembly

1. Let the print cool. Support the holder at its solid corners and cut the six small base ties. The two ties nearest the battery entrance meet the corner post. Keep the thin rails that retain the battery.
2. Slide the holder onto the 8 mm rod. Clip the split grip around the rod behind the holder and push its tapered end into the sleeve. Withdraw the flange to readjust it.
3. Leave the black mouse switch off the OP1. Bring the board up beside the rod at the open end with its white connector and lead attached. Slide the board along the holder from underneath, white connector first. Set it down onto its supports. Plug the black switch into the board.
4. Fit the two-post bar to the paddle, plug in the touch board before lowering it, and keep its white socket facing down. Route the three wires over the lowered pivot and out the side. Slide one board clip over each exposed post.
5. Lower the paddle into the two hinge seats. Cut its two disposable pad ties flush, then insert the standard pin through the three aligned holes with its flat facing down. Slide the centre clip into the pin groove from the rear. The kit includes a spare centre clip and a second, slightly thicker pin for an alternative fit. Hold the paddle down and slide the flat OP1 retainer into its upper rails.
6. Fit the wired key sockets from their indicated open sides. The two flat backing strips support the lower sockets while the keys are pressed home. Remove each strip after seating its switch. The integrated battery slides against the underside of the key deck with its connector outside.
7. Press and release the pad at the centre and near each front corner. Check the clip, swivel, pin, wire bend and rod grip in the assembled holder. For a precise click without moving the pointer, press higher up, outside the active touch area.
