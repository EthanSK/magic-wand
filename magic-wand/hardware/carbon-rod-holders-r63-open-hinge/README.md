# R63 key-holder replacement

[Key assembly](../carbon-rod-holders/assembly/?version=r63&component=keys) · [Battery](../carbon-rod-holders/assembly/?version=r63&component=battery) · [Full 500 mm rod](../carbon-rod-holders/assembly/?version=r63&component=whole)

The replacement kit contains the key holder, touch paddle, left socket backing strip and split rod grip. Reuse the R62 standard 3 mm hinge pin, centre retaining clip, OP1 retainer, DFRobot post bar and board clips. The R62 spare centre clip and right socket backing strip remain compatible.

Let the plate cool. At the battery entrance, cut the two thin base ties flush with side cutters while supporting the two battery lips. Cut the remaining ties before peeling away the base. Lift the paddle and its barrel together.

## Assembly

1. Bring the black socket for the key with the turned mounting holes in from the open outside edge. Both metal switch contacts face that edge. Lift the socket into position. Slide the left backing strip under it while pressing the switch home, then withdraw the strip. The other three switches keep their R62 orientations.
2. Seat the paddle's round barrel on the two rests between the hinge ears. With the paddle down, push the standard pin through both ears and the barrel. Press the centre clip into the groove in the pin. Check that the pin stays in and the paddle clicks and returns.
3. Slide the wrapped battery lengthwise along the underside of the keypad. Its broad face rests against the base and the side spring retains it. Keep the connector outside the holder. Pull the exposed end of the pack for removal.
4. Slip the holder over the 8 mm carbon tube and slide it to the desired position. Clip the split grip around the rod behind the holder. Push its tapered end into the holder until it holds position. Pull the flange out to loosen the grip before moving the holder.

## Fit checks

The R62 physical assembly blocked both the hinge pin and the carbon rod. R63 aligns the two ears and the barrel on one common pin axis and provides a separate grip for the enlarged carbon-rod passage. The R63 battery seat extends 4.90 mm below the keypad deck at the pack, compared with 12.97 mm in the previous design. The nominal broad-face gap dropped from 7.97 mm to 0.05 mm.

The tests check the complete pin insertion line, the full rod passage, the swept socket and backing-strip paths, the battery seat, the spring and the paddle travel. Rod gripping force, hinge effort, switch retention and battery removal force require a fitted physical assembly.

Both the original and reopened slices clear all 62 tested paths, with 3.309 mm through the assembled hinge and 8.297 mm along the sleeve. The six detachable ties contact 21.45 mm² of the carrier and leave the hinge bay clear. Opus approved one monitored trial. Studio flags possible floating regions on the carrier. The short one-ended extrusions are at the rod socket mouth and keypad deck. Every printed region starts on the bed or on material from a preceding layer.

The A1 mini finished the R63 replacement on 24 November 2026. The camera showed all four parts still attached to the plate after 312 layers. The hinge pin, installed paddle click, carbon rod and grip, battery retention, and outside socket insertion still need a hands-on fit check.
