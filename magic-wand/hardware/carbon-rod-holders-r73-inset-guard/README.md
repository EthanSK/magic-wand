# R73 key holder

[Open the R73 key holder in the assembly viewer](../carbon-rod-holders/assembly/?version=r73&component=keys).

The battery-side outer finger guard now follows the side curve of the other three guards into the rounded bottom edge. Its two roots remain 14 mm apart to support the finger. The new branch bends sideways to leave room to slide the wired black socket in from outside. The battery remains beside the carbon rod.

The old tip projected 2.74 mm beyond the opposite rim. A check across the full 2.7 mm width of the revised root measured less than 0.001 mm of outward projection in the exported mesh. The minimum measured section along the revised branch is 4.98 mm². These are model measurements. Comfort, fit and strength in printed PETG still need a physical trial.
