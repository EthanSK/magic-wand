# R75 click-end finger rest

[See the finger rest from underneath](../carbon-rod-holders/assembly/?version=r75&component=finger_rest) · [Open the complete key assembly](../carbon-rod-holders/assembly/?version=r75&component=keys)

The click end is opposite the battery opening. Two rounded branches carry the index finger to the lower rim. The branch toward the rod passes over the battery exit. The outside branch turns beneath the sideways-loading socket. The fork joins the plate between the keys. The matching pair on the other bank supplies the shape reference.

The battery slot uses two shallow upper lips in place of the three tall spacers. A lip is 0.90 mm thick. The pouch retains its through path and side spring. In the nominal assembly the soldered terminal and wire space extends to within 0.20 mm of the pouch. The actual wrap, solder joints, friction and comfort need a fitted trial.

The key seats have level undersides wherever the modelled hardware can clear them. The checked openings include the locating pins, socket bosses and OP1 plug route.
