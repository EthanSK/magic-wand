How do I open a HUD page and go back?

Some cards open their own page, like Keys mode, Utility modes or controls for the current app.
Outline one and tap Middle to open its page.
The page's exit card takes you back one page.
Double tap Middle at any time to close the whole HUD.

Steps:
1. Tap Middle
2. Outline a page card
3. Tap to open
4. Exit goes back
5. Double tap to close
