How do I review my changes in VS Code?

In VS Code, tap Middle to open the HUD. Review changes is just right of Switch App.
Outline it and tap Middle. The HUD hides, the light turns green and review starts.
With no button held, circle clockwise for the next change and anticlockwise for the previous one.
After a pause, a quick nudge down or up moves one change forward or back.
Files you finish join the set. Hold Middle for about half a second to stage them and keep reviewing.
Tap Middle once to cancel and leave review.

Steps:
1. Open the HUD
2. Choose Review changes
3. Circle to move
4. Nudge to step
5. Hold Middle to stage
6. Tap Middle to exit
