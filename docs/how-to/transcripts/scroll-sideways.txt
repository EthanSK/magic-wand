How do I scroll sideways?

Hold Bottom and start by moving left or right. That picks sideways scrolling.
Carry on in circles to keep scrolling the same way. It stays sideways until you let go.
Reverse the circles to scroll back, then release Bottom to stop.

Steps:
1. Hold Bottom
2. Move left or right
3. Keep circling
4. Reverse to go back
5. Release to stop
