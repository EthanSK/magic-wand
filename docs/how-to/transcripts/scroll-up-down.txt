How do I scroll up and down?

Hold Bottom and move the wand up or down. That picks vertical scrolling, and the page follows the wand.
To keep going, carry on in circles. Your first loop can go either way round.
Reverse your circles to reverse the scrolling.
Release Bottom to stop. The pointer then follows the wand.

Steps:
1. Hold Bottom
2. Move up or down
3. Keep circling
4. Reverse to go back
5. Release to stop
