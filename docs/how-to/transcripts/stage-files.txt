How do I stage files in VS Code?

In VS Code, open the HUD, outline Stage files next to Review changes, and tap Middle. The light turns purple.
A clockwise quarter-turn moves to the next file. Turn anticlockwise to go back one file.
Each quarter-turn moves one file. Two clockwise quarter-turns move forward two. Reverse to go back one.
Hold Middle for about half a second to stage the whole set. You stay in Stage files, ready for the next one.
Tap Middle once to cancel and leave.

Steps:
1. Open the HUD
2. Choose Stage files
3. Circle to select
4. Quarter-turn to step
5. Hold Middle to stage
6. Tap Middle to exit
