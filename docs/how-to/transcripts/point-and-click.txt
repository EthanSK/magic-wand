How do I move the pointer and click?

Tap Bottom once. The light turns blue and the pointer starts following the wand. Nothing is clicked.
Turn the wand to move the pointer.
Stop on what you want. After a brief pause, it clicks once.
Tap Bottom again to stop following, without a click.

Steps:
1. Tap Bottom
2. Turn to point
3. Stop to click
4. Tap Bottom to stop
