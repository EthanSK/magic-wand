How do I open the HUD and use a card?

Tap Middle. The HUD opens on Switch App, and the light turns orange.
Turn the wand to move the outline. It stops at the edges.
Tap Middle again to use the outlined card. The HUD stays open.
Double tap Middle to close the HUD.
For a quick one-off, hold Middle, turn to a card and release to use it.

Steps:
1. Tap Middle
2. Turn to choose
3. Tap to use
4. Double tap to close
5. Or hold, turn, release
