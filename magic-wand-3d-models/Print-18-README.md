# Print V18 — direct seated handle and slimmer grip

Both removable handle ends now run straight into matching seats in the wand. The lower U return, rounded mounting bulb and exposed thin-pin gap are removed. Each end has a 14.4 × 9.6 mm rounded shoulder entering a shallow 1.2 mm recess, a 4.2 mm diameter neck and a 7.2 mm wide captured head. The upper and lower shoulders use the same geometry. All pegs and release hardware stay on the removable handle; the bare wand has recessed openings.

The grip core diameter is **1.6 mm smaller than V17** through the hand area: about 25.4 mm at the lower mount, 24.7 mm at 64 mm height and 22.5 mm at the upper mount, excluding the preserved raised helix. The profile eases into the unchanged 30 mm battery cap. The lower shaft meets the slimmer grip with a continuous taper; the upper shaft and diamond retain their established sizes.

**Reprint one handed stem, the removable loop and the matching lower shaft.** The larger seated mounts do not fit older stems or loops. The complete project contains both handed stems and one shared set of the remaining parts; choose one stem per wand. Earlier revisions are unchanged. The loop-only file is an optional subset, not an upgrade compatible with V17 stems.

## Attaching the handle

Viewed from the tip towards the grip, start with the handle 12° clockwise from its final position. Insert both pegs and shoulders into their recessed entries, lift 4 mm, then turn left 12° along the 0.6 mm cam rise. Let the reverse-turn catch seat. To remove, press the release tab outward, turn right, lower 4 mm and withdraw. The broad shoulders seat inside the wand instead of leaving the handle body floating away from it. The nominal clearance is 0.22 mm; cam relief and spring preload accommodate movement. A zero-clearance printed fit would jam.

Geometry checks cover both handed stems, the full insertion/turn/removal routes, separate radial/up/down stops, the reverse catch, preload throughout free radial travel, matching shoulders, cap/button clearance, wire routes and a bare wand with no projecting mounts. They do not establish printed strength, PETG fatigue, fit force or hand comfort. Print the small fit test first.

## Retained features and print estimates

The 410 mm wand keeps its original counter-wound raised helix, Art Nouveau openings, rounded diamond, clipped Sense cradle, upward USB access, rear solder space, captured cover, 10.4 mm main wire bore, thin battery guides, recessed switch mounts and flat switch plates. Mounting holes locally interrupt the shell where required. Button heights remain 139 / 135 / 109 mm. The battery envelope remains provisional at 20 × 6 × 40 mm.

Seven prepared projects use P1S 0.4 mm PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls and 20% infill. The loop prints on its broad face with manual support at the roots; switch plates print broad face down. One left wand estimates **72.411 g printed parts**, plus **2.338 g removable supports/brim**, compared with V17's 75.048 g printed parts. Electronics, battery, wires, switches, sockets and caps are additional and unweighed. The whole assembled wand's 80 g target is not yet verified.
