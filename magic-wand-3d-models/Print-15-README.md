# Print V15 — recessed handle lock, both hands

The wand stays usable with the loop removed: its receivers are recessed within the original surface. The enlarged pegs, roots and release tab all leave with the loop. Only small recessed keyhole and catch openings remain. V14’s projecting housings are superseded.

Insert at a 12° clockwise offset, lift 4 mm, turn left 12° through a 0.6 mm cam rise, then release the catch. Directions are viewed from the tip toward the grip. For removal, press the tab outward, reverse the turn, lower 4 mm and withdraw the pegs. Use the matching V15 stem and loop; print the small test first. Physical fit, force and strength remain unverified.

One shared set of remaining parts is included in the complete project; choose one stem.

The full original raised helix, taper, Art Nouveau windows, final loop pose, controls, board cradle, USB, cap and wire routes are retained. Thirteen shared meshes match V14 exactly. One left wand estimates **76.139 g printed PETG**, with 4.199 g removable support/brim. Hardware is unweighed; the allowance to a 100 g complete wand is 23.861 g. Geometry and slicer checks do not establish physical acceptance.
