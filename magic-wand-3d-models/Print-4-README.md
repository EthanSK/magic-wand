The original counter-wound helix is restored. The core narrows continuously toward a matching head neck; reinforcement stays inside the silhouette. Long shaft spans have 1.2 mm skins with a minimum 10.4 mm wire route. Both lower-shaft choices make a 410 mm wand, 70 mm longer than V2. The new grip joins at 160 mm; the retained V2 grip joins at 140 mm. Do not mix the two lower shafts.

Matched PETG slicer estimates, including supports/brims: new left grip **49.16 g versus 76.89 g** (36.1% less); longer upper set **44.18 g versus 49.42 g** (10.6% less); complete fourteen-instance plate **146.13 g versus 213.49 g** (31.6% less). These are slicer estimates, not weighed prints. The complete plate is approximately 6 h 25 min at the recorded settings.

The compact recessed inserts retain the purchased Choc socket/post holes. Raised mirrored buttons, hollow loop/grip, removable bottom cap, open battery sled, three twist joints, rounded diamond, captured sliding cover and clipped Sense cradle are retained. Both handles use normal supports; sampled sliced paths protect their internal wiring and battery space. No unsupported-region or floating-cantilever warning appears in the prepared slices.

The battery envelope remains provisional, 20 × 6 × 40 mm. Physical fit, clip force, PETG strength, switch travel and comfort still need trials. No printing has been started. Projects use P1S 0.4 mm, PETG, 0.2 mm layers, three walls and 20% infill; select the actual loaded printer/filament before use.
