# Print V7 — slimmer ventilated wand, both hands

The 410 mm helix wand now has a 1.6 mm slimmer grip, an 18 × 12 mm hollow loop, and flute-like ventilation holes in the grip, loop and shafts. Thin hollow skins and local reinforcement replace unnecessary material. Button centres, slide-in plates, joints and diamond silhouette are retained.

One complete left wand estimates **75.688 g printed PETG**, down from 91.027 g in V6 (**16.85% less**). The grip alone falls from 45.908 to 35.859 g. Allow a further **24.312 g maximum** for all bought hardware to remain at 100 g; that hardware is unweighed. An 80 g fully assembled wand is not established. Removable support/brim is excluded from finished-part mass.
