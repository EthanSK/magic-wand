# Print V14 — lift, turn left and lock

[Both handed stems plus shared remaining parts](Print-14-complete-left-right-top-slide-410mm.3mf) · [one left wand](Print-14-one-left-wand-410mm.3mf) · [small cam-lock test](Print-14-detachable-loop-fit-test.3mf) · [editable CAD and guide](../magic-wand/hardware/printed-loop-wand/v14/README.md)

Align the loop 12° clockwise from its final position, lift 22 mm, turn left 12° along a 0.6 mm cam rise, and let the spring catch seat. Directions are viewed from the tip toward the grip. To remove, press the tab outward, reverse the turn and lower the loop. Closed upper stops carry upright load; the locked bayonet also captures a straight downward pull. Radial spring preload is designed to take up play. Print the small test first; printed force and tolerances remain unverified.

**Use the matching V14 stem and loop.** Both handed stems are included, with one shared set of remaining parts. The shafts, head/cover, cap and other shared meshes are unchanged from V13. Final loop pose, hollow leaf shell, full helix, taper, controls and wire/battery routes are retained.

One left wand estimates **77.003 g printed PETG**, up **0.285 g** from V13. Support/brim adds 4.619 g; hardware is unweighed, with 22.997 g allowance to the 100 g ceiling. Geometry, movement, sliced-path checks and six warning-free project slices are recorded. These do not certify strength, spring life, physical fit or complete weight.
