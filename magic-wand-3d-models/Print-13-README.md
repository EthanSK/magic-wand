# Print V13 — loop attaches upward, releases downward

[Complete project: both handed stems plus shared remaining parts](Print-13-complete-left-right-top-slide-410mm.3mf) · [one left wand](Print-13-one-left-wand-410mm.3mf) · [editable CAD and full guide](../magic-wand/hardware/printed-loop-wand/v13/README.md)

The detachable loop slides upward 22 mm to attach, seating against closed upper stops under normal tip-up wand load. The reversed side tongue prevents downward withdrawal. Press it outward, slide the loop downward and move it away to remove it. Wiring stays in the stem. **Use the V13 stem and loop together; V12 mounts do not interchange.**

Both hands and the small [mount fit test](Print-13-detachable-loop-fit-test.3mf) are included. Try the coupon first. V12's leaf openings, reinforced rails, helix, taper, controls and other interfaces remain. Shared shafts/head/cap have unchanged mesh hashes.

One left wand estimates **76.718 g printed PETG**, up **0.843 g** from V12; supports/brim add 4.872 g. Hardware is unweighed, with 23.282 g allowance to the 100 g ceiling. Geometry, sampled extrusion and six warning-free slices are checked; physical strength, printed fit, latch life and all-up mass are unverified.
