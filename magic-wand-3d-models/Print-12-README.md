# Print V12 — curved leaves and fuller rails, both hands

[Complete project: both handed stems plus shared remaining parts](Print-12-complete-left-right-top-slide-410mm.3mf) · [one left wand](Print-12-one-left-wand-410mm.3mf) · [editable CAD and guide](../magic-wand/hardware/printed-loop-wand/v12/README.md)

Smaller curved leaf openings and softened intersections refine V11 in an Art Nouveau direction. Rails widen to 3.6 mm and deepen to 1.6 mm in the grip and 1.8 mm in the shafts; their inside corners are rounded. The loop skin is 1.45 mm, with smaller curved ports. The complete spiral, taper, length, buttons, board/battery/wire routes and all mechanical interfaces are retained.

One left wand estimates **75.875 g printed PETG**, **5.121 g more than V11**; supports/brim add 4.465 g. Hardware is unweighed, with 24.125 g allowance to the 100 g ceiling. All six projects slice without reported warnings; mesh, fit, layer and protected-path checks pass. Physical strength, fit and all-up weight remain unverified.

The complete plate contains both handed stems and one shared set of other parts. Choose one stem. Optional [upper replacement for a retained V2 grip](Print-12-upper-only-helix-410mm.3mf). Previous releases are unchanged.
