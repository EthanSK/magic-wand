# Print V8 — lower ergonomic loop and higher controls, both hands

[Open the complete prepared Bambu project](Print-8-complete-left-right-top-slide-410mm.3mf): **11 build instances**, both handed grips plus one shared set of the remaining parts. Choose one grip for a working wand. The optional [single left wand](Print-8-one-left-wand-410mm.3mf) has 10 instances.

The loop now sits at the butt with cap clearance. All three controls move up 10 mm, retaining their spacing and angles. The upper mug arm slopes down away from its high index-side root. Four loop openings reach the actual inner finger-contact surface, with additional contact-facing grip outlets. The 410 mm taper, crossed helix, thin top-slide Choc plates, captured diamond cover, board retention and cable routes remain.

One left wand estimates **75.821 g of printed PETG**, plus 3.886 g of discarded support/brim (79.80 g total consumed filament including startup/rounding). This is about 0.13 g above V7. Hardware is unweighed: 24.179 g remains for electronics, battery, switches, caps and wiring to meet the 100 g assembled ceiling. Under 80 g assembled is not established.
