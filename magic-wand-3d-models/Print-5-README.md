A rounded **16.4 × 17.2 mm plate, 1.3 mm thick**, slides down from above into guides built directly into the wand. Two small rear feet bring its total thickness to 2.5 mm. The separate cassette and keeper are removed. The bought socket sits in the backed wand recess; after sliding the plate down, press in the bought switch and keycap. No glue or extra printed cap. The socket's wire outlet is 6.4 mm; the 14 mm plate opening is occupied by the switch. Try the small coupon before the full grip.

The V4 helix, continuous taper, hollow internals, 410 mm length, both handed grips, button positions, three bayonets, rounded diamond and captured head cover remain. The complete plate has both grips; choose one for a working wand.

PETG slicer estimates at 0.2 mm, three walls and 20% infill on P1S 0.4 mm. Removable support is separated from the parts; electronics, battery and bought hardware are excluded. The original V1 archive used 0.28 mm/two walls and estimated 112.89 g including support; that is a different print setup. The complete V5 plate with **both** grips uses about 148.6 g and must not be called one wand's weight. See the [machine-readable comparison](../magic-wand/hardware/printed-loop-wand/v5/exports/one-wand-mass-comparison.json).

All prepared slices have no floating-region/unsupported warning. The small receiver coupon needs accessible normal supports; remove them before testing. Geometry and sliced-path checks do not establish printed PETG fit, clip force or strength. Battery fit remains provisional. No printer job has been started.
