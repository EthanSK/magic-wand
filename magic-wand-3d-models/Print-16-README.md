# Print V16 — borderless recessed switches, both hands

The square switch surrounds are removed. All three switch plates and bought sockets sit 3.4 mm deeper in the curved stem, held by small side clips and lower stops. The bought keycaps cover the thin plates. Plates now print broad face flat on the bed, 2.5 mm high instead of 17.2 mm upright. There are no extra printed keycaps or keeper boxes.

[Complete project — both handed stems](Print-16-complete-left-right-recessed-switch-410mm.3mf), [one left wand](Print-16-one-left-wand-410mm.3mf), [small switch-pocket test](Print-16-recessed-switch-button-fit-test.3mf), and [editable CAD and assembly guide](../magic-wand/hardware/printed-loop-wand/v16/README.md). Print the small test first; clip stiffness, fit and fatigue are unverified. The complete project includes one shared set of remaining parts; choose one stem.

The helix, taper, Art Nouveau openings, control heights/angles, rounded diamond, electronics routes and recessed detachable-loop lock remain. Fourteen shared meshes match V15. One left wand estimates 75.226 g printed PETG plus 3.632 g removable support/brim. Hardware is unweighed. Prior files remain unchanged.
